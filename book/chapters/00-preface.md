# Preface

*A report on laser dry-fire training as it stood in the fourth quarter of 2026, built from an extensive search of vendor documentation, owner reports and published training books.*

No product in this report was bought, borrowed or tested. Every statement about a product comes from what its maker publishes, checked against the vendor's own pages rather than taken from a review.

Two published training books supply the method the technology is measured against: Ben Stoeger's *Dryfire Reloaded* and Chris Cheng's *Shoot to Win*.<sup>[1, 2]</sup> They were written before any of this software existed, which makes them a useful standard rather than a competing product claim.

No prices appear anywhere in this report. Amounts in this category move faster than any report can, so products are described by how they charge instead: free, paid once, subscription, free with paid unlocks, or bundled with hardware. That shape outlives any particular figure, and it is the part that decides what you still own in three years.

Everything described here is the state of the market in October 2026. Several of these products are young, and young products change.
